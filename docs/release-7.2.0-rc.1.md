# 7.2.0-rc.1

Local candidate for generic persistent upload pages, authoritative topology journals, regional sparse-source queries and the RGB channel-maximum executor. Adds compatible default spatial-query and spatial-index statistics.

The candidate retains the 7.1.7 NVIDIA native bridge to keep dependency bytes fixed. Raw GI and colored transmission match the disabled-path reference in 54 HDR captures; actual GI RR matches in another 54 captures at 192×128. The first 33×25 RR fixture failed because its extent was too small and is retained as failed evidence.

Actual host throughput, dense-region navigation cost and shadow-ray increases are still under evaluation. This candidate does not claim a measured FPS gain or completion of the 4 ms budget. Source and final release publication remain pending acceptance; binaries are local only.
